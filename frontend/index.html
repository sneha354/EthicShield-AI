The 5 Pillars of Ethical AI Auditing

⚖️

1. Fairness & Bias

Detects algorithmic discrimination, demographic disparity, and violation of the Four-Fifths (80%) Rule across sensitive protected groups (gender, race, age).

🔍

2. Explainability

Quantifies model interpretability using SHAP feature attributions, surrogate decision tree fidelity, and checks against single-feature dominance.

🔒

3. Privacy Protection

Scans datasets for Personally Identifiable Information (PII) using Microsoft Presidio, assesses k-anonymity linkage risk, and detects membership inference vulnerability.

🛡️

4. Robustness & Safety

Evaluates stability under adversarial input noise jitter, missing-data dropout, extreme out-of-distribution (OOD) boundaries, and anomaly distributions.

📜

5. Transparency

Automatically drafts standardized Model Cards and Dataset Datasheets, validating documentation completeness against EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF guidelines.
